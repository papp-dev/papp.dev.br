Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how P A P P DESENVOLVIMENTO DE SOFTWARE LTDA ("PAPP DEV"), Brazilian company tax ID (CNPJ) 41.794.829/0001-90, located at Rua Jose Antonio Ferrari, 262, Parque Industrial, Cianorte – PR, 87207-030, Brazil, processes personal data in the apps it publishes and on this website, in accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 – LGPD). PAPP DEV is the data controller for the data processed in its apps.

This policy applies to all of our apps. Each app also has its own page, listed below, describing the specific data it collects, how it is used and who the app is for. Where they differ, the app's page prevails for that app.

[Our principles]

  • We collect only the data each app needs to work.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not send personal data to artificial intelligence or machine learning tools or platforms without disclosing it in this policy and asking for your consent first.
  • Sensitive data, such as health data, is only processed with specific and explicit consent.

[This website]

This website is informational. It does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools, and it does not collect personal data, except what you voluntarily send when contacting us by email or WhatsApp.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs technical access data, such as IP addresses, to operate and protect the service. The website's font is loaded from Google Fonts, which also receives your browser's IP address when serving the font. This data is processed by GitHub and Google under their own privacy policies; PAPP DEV has no access to it.

[Infrastructure providers]

Our apps may use cloud, database, storage and email services (for example, Apple iCloud/CloudKit, Google or server providers) that act on our behalf, under contract and without the right to use the data for their own purposes. We may also share data when required by law or court order.

[International transfers]

Some providers may store data on servers outside Brazil. In these cases, the transfer follows the LGPD, using providers that offer adequate data protection safeguards.

[Storage and security]

Data is stored with restricted access and encrypted in transit and at rest. We keep data while your account is active. After you delete your account or withdraw your consent, we delete your data within 30 days, except for what the law requires us to keep.

[Your rights]

You can request confirmation of processing, access, correction, portability, anonymization or deletion of your data, information about sharing, and withdraw your consent at any time. In apps with accounts, you can delete your account in the app or by emailing the data protection officer below.

[Children and teenagers]

We only process children's data in apps designed for children or their parents, always in the child's best interest and with the specific consent of at least one parent or legal guardian, as required by Article 14 of the LGPD. Each app's rules are described on its page.

[Changes]

We may update this policy and the app pages. Relevant changes will be communicated in the app or on this page, with the new update date. Each app's data details also appear in the App Store privacy labels and in the Google Play Data safety section.
