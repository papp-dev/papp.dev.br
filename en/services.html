[Services]

Custom software development, from the first version to maintenance in production. We work with companies that need a new system, an app in the stores, or someone to take over existing code.

[Web systems]

Applications that run in the browser and the backend behind them. We build with Node.js, TypeScript and React, on relational databases and cloud hosting.

  • Admin dashboards and internal management systems
  • Customer portals, logged-in areas and self-service
  • SaaS platforms with plans, subscriptions and multiple companies
  • Reports, exports and dashboards
  • Role-based access control and audit logs

[Mobile apps]

Apps for iPhone and Android, with a single codebase when it makes sense or native when the project requires it. We take care of the whole path to the store.

  • iOS and Android development
  • Login, push notifications, in-app payments and offline use
  • Publishing on the App Store and Google Play, including store listings, privacy labels and store review
  • Updates for new iOS and Android versions
  • Backend and admin dashboard for the app

[Integrations and APIs]

We get different systems talking to each other and take repetitive tasks off people's hands.

  • Payment gateways, Pix, boleto and cards; payment reconciliation
  • Brazilian electronic invoicing (NF-e)
  • Integration with ERPs, CRMs and legacy systems
  • Documented REST APIs for partners and for your own apps
  • Scheduled jobs, file imports and webhooks

[Maintenance and evolution]

We take over systems that are already in production, including those built by other teams.

  • Assessment of the code, the infrastructure and current risks
  • Bug fixes and performance improvements
  • Dependency upgrades and cloud migration
  • New features with monthly follow-up
