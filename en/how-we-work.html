[How we work]

A simple process, agreed in writing, in which you follow what is being built from start to finish.

[Steps]

01
Discovery
A free conversation to understand the problem, who will use the system, the deadline and what needs to be ready first.
02
Proposal
Scope, estimated timeline, price and billing agreed in writing before we start. Nothing is charged beyond what was agreed.
03
Development
Short, frequent deliveries. You get access to a test version, follow its progress and can adjust priorities along the way.
04
Delivery and support
Release to production or to the stores, with source code, documentation and access handed over to you. Ongoing maintenance when the project calls for it.

[Engagement models]

Fixed-scope project
Defined scope, fixed price and timeline. Best for a first version or a well-defined deliverable.
Monthly hours
A monthly bank of hours to evolve and maintain a system, with priorities set by you.
Support
Monitoring, fixes and updates to keep a published system or app running.

[FAQ]

Who owns the code?

The client does. At the end of the project we hand over the source code, the documentation and all access (servers, app store accounts, domains).

Do you work with companies in other cities and outside Brazil?

Yes. We work remotely and serve clients across Brazil and in the United States, in Portuguese or English.

Do you take over a system built by another team?

Yes. We start with an assessment of the code and infrastructure and show you what needs attention before proposing changes.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After the first conversation we send a written proposal with price and timeline, with no commitment.

Do you publish the app on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We prepare the store listing and the privacy labels, and follow the review until the app is approved, under the client's account or ours.
