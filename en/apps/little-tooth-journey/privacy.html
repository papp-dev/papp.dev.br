Privacy — Little Tooth Journey

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This page complements the PAPP DEV general privacy policy and describes how Little Tooth Journey, our app that helps parents and caregivers follow a child's oral health from pregnancy to the permanent teeth, processes personal data.

In short: the app has no sign-up or login. The data you record stays on your iPhone and, if you turn on sync, in your private iCloud account. PAPP DEV does not receive and has no access to this data.

[Who the app is for]

The app is intended for adults who care for a child, such as parents, grandparents or guardians, who record information about the child in their care, including during pregnancy. The child does not use the app directly, and dentists and clinics are not users of the app. The app is available for iPhone.

[Data we collect]

The app does not ask for any data about you: there is no sign-up, email, phone number or password. The data below is entered by you, about the child, and stays under your control:

  • Child details: name (optional during pregnancy), date of birth or due date, gender (with a "Prefer not to say" option) and, if you wish, a photo.
  • Oral health and development data: milestones and the dates they happened (such as a tooth coming in or falling out), brushing records, weeks of pregnancy, whether the baby was born premature and any notes you write.
  • Milestone photos and videos: if you add them, up to 5 photos and 2 videos per milestone. Videos are stored as they were recorded and may contain metadata, such as location, if your device recorded it.
  • Dental appointments: date, time, type of appointment, name of the dentist and clinic, and optional notes.
  • App preferences: reminder times, language, selected child, date you accepted the terms and whether iCloud sync is on.

The app does not collect location, contacts, audio, payment data or advertising identifiers, and it does not use analytics, tracking or crash reporting tools.

Oral health, pregnancy and prematurity data, the child's photos and videos, and appointment notes are sensitive personal data (health-related) and children's data under the LGPD. They should only be recorded with the specific and explicit consent of a parent or legal guardian, given in the app before the first record [CONFIRMAR: the app does not yet have a specific and explicit consent screen; today it only records acceptance of the terms when "Get started" is tapped]. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the data, as explained under [Deleting your data].

[Device permissions]

  • Camera: to take photos of the child and of milestones in the app. Your iPhone asks for this permission the first time you choose to take a photo.
  • Photos: the app uses the iPhone's built-in photo picker, which gives the app only the photos and videos you select. That is why the app does not ask for access to your photo library.
  • Notifications: for brushing, appointment and milestone reminders. These are local notifications, created on the device itself. Brushing reminders show the child's name and may appear on the lock screen. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in your iPhone's settings.
  • iCloud: only if you turn on sync. In that case, the app also receives silent system notifications to keep the data up to date across your devices.

[How we use data]

The data is used only inside the app, to:

  • Show the child's timeline and milestones;
  • Record brushing and appointments;
  • Send the reminders you turn on;
  • Sync the data across your devices, if you turn on iCloud.

The data is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sent to artificial intelligence tools or platforms.

[Sharing]

  • PAPP DEV: does not receive the data. The app has no server of its own and does not use third-party SDKs for analytics, ads, crash reporting, login or payments.
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit): only if you turn on sync. The data is stored in your Apple account's private database, which no one else, including PAPP DEV, can access. This storage is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and the servers may be located outside Brazil.
  • Exported file: if you use "Export as JSON", you decide who to share the file with.
  • External links: the app links to scientific references on third-party websites (such as PubMed Central and SciELO). When you open them, those websites' policies apply.

[Where data is stored]

  • On your iPhone, inside the app. This is the default.
  • In your private iCloud, if you turn on "Sync via iCloud". Sync is off by default.
  • Photos and videos stay on the device only and are not sent to iCloud by the app.
  • If iPhone backup is on, the app's data may be included in it, depending on your Apple account settings.

[Children]

Little Tooth Journey processes children's data, including health data, and therefore follows Article 14 of the LGPD:

  • Consent is given by a parent or legal guardian before anything is recorded about the child.
  • We collect only the minimum needed for the app to work. Photo, gender, notes, milestone photos and videos, and appointment notes are optional.
  • The parent or guardian can view, export, correct and delete the data at any time, in the app itself.
  • The app has no ads, purchases, analytics or tracking.

[Deleting your data]

  • One child: when you delete a child, they stay in "Recently deleted" for 30 days and can still be restored. After that, the child's data, photos, videos and reminders are permanently deleted.
  • Individual items: brushing records, appointments, milestones and attachments can be deleted one by one.
  • Everything: Settings → Data → "Delete everything" deletes all of the app's data and files from the device.
  • iCloud: Settings → Sync → iCloud → "Delete iCloud-synced data" removes the data from your iCloud account. If you use sync, use this option together with "Delete everything".
  • Copy of your data: Settings → Data → "Export as JSON" creates a file with the recorded data (photos and videos are not included in the file).

Uninstalling the app also deletes the data from the device, but not the data already synced to iCloud. Since PAPP DEV does not receive the data, we cannot view or delete it for you: deletion is done in the app itself. For questions, contact our data protection officer.
