Privacy — PAPP Coach

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This page complements the PAPP DEV general privacy policy and describes how PAPP Coach, our running and triathlon training prescription app, processes personal data.

[Who the app is for]

The app connects coaches with the athletes they train. It is intended for people aged 18 or older. Athletes under 18 may only use it with the authorization and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

[Data we collect]

  • Account data: name, email and, when needed for training follow-up, information such as date of birth, sex, weight and height.
  • Training and physical activity data: prescribed and completed workouts, distance, duration, pace, speed, cadence, power, heart rate, elevation and routes with GPS location.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: app crashes and performance, without identifying you personally.

Heart rate and other physiological metrics are sensitive personal data (health-related) under the LGPD. We only process this data with your specific and explicit consent, requested in the app before any collection, and you can withdraw it at any time.

[Garmin integration]

The app can integrate with your Garmin Connect account. The connection only happens if you authorize it through Garmin's own authorization screen (OAuth). We never receive your Garmin password.

With your authorization:

  • We receive from Garmin the activities you record on your device (for example: activity type, date, duration, distance, pace, heart rate and GPS route).
  • We send to Garmin the structured workouts prescribed for you, so they appear on your watch's calendar.

You can disconnect the integration at any time, in the app or in your Garmin Connect account settings. When you disconnect, we stop receiving data from Garmin and delete the data received from Garmin within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Data received from Garmin is used only to provide the app's features to you and your coach, and is not sent to artificial intelligence or machine learning tools or platforms. Garmin and Garmin Connect are trademarks of Garmin Ltd. or its subsidiaries; PAPP DEV is not affiliated with Garmin.

[How we use data]

  • So that your coach can prescribe workouts, follow completed sessions, compare planned and executed training, and adjust your plan.
  • To provide and maintain the app, provide support and fix errors.

[Sharing]

  • Your coach: training and activity data is shared with the coach you are linked to in the app, which is the purpose of the service.
  • Garmin: only the prescribed workouts sent to your calendar, as described above.
  • Infrastructure providers: as described in the general policy.
