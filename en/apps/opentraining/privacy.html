Privacy — OpenTraining

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This page complements the PAPP DEV general privacy policy and describes how OpenTraining, our running and triathlon training planning and tracking platform, processes personal data. P A P P DESENVOLVIMENTO DE SOFTWARE LTDA ("PAPP DEV") is the controller of this data.

[Who the app is for]

OpenTraining connects coaches with the athletes they train. Coaches create their coaching team and invite athletes; athletes receive planned workouts and upload the activities they complete. The service is available as iOS and Android apps and on the web.

The app is intended for people aged 18 or older. Athletes under 18 may only use it with the authorization and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, and it is not intended for children under 13.

[Data we collect]

  • Account data: name, email and password. Your password is never stored in plain text: we only store a hash (Argon2), which cannot be reversed.
  • Login sessions: the device and the browser or app used (user agent) for each session, so you can see where you are signed in and end sessions.
  • Training data: scheduled workouts, training thresholds and zones (for example pace, heart rate and power) and comments exchanged between athlete and coach.
  • Activities uploaded by the athlete: FIT or GPX files of completed activities, which may contain routes with GPS location, heart rate, power, cadence, distance, duration and pace.

Heart rate, power and other physiological metrics are sensitive personal data (health-related) under the LGPD, and GPS routes reveal where you train. We only process this data with your specific and explicit consent, requested in the app before your first activity (file upload, manual entry or Garmin), and you can withdraw it at any time. If you withdraw it, we stop receiving new activities from you; the activities already recorded are kept until you ask us to delete them.

OpenTraining does not use third-party analytics, telemetry or tracking tools, does not show ads and does not collect advertising identifiers.

[Biometrics]

If you turn on unlocking with Face ID or fingerprint, the check is performed by your device's operating system. The app only receives confirmation that the check succeeded. PAPP DEV never receives or stores biometric data.

[Garmin integration]

Athletes can connect their Garmin Connect account to OpenTraining. The connection is optional and only happens if you authorize it through Garmin's own authorization screen; we never ask for or receive your Garmin password.

With your authorization:

  • We receive from Garmin the running activities you record on your device, including the activity file (FIT) with date, duration, distance, pace, heart rate, cadence, elevation and GPS route.
  • We send to Garmin the structured workouts your coach schedules for you, so they appear on your Garmin Connect calendar and on your watch.

Data received from Garmin is used only to show your activities to you and to your coach, and to compare planned and completed training. It is stored on the server operated by PAPP DEV in Brazil, is never sold, rented or shared with third parties, is not sent to artificial intelligence or machine learning tools or platforms, and is never used to train, evaluate or improve AI models.

You can disconnect at any time in the app or in your Garmin Connect account settings. When you disconnect, we stop receiving data from Garmin and delete the data received from Garmin within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it. You can also request access, correction, export or deletion of this data as described in "Your rights".

Garmin and Garmin Connect are trademarks of Garmin Ltd. or its subsidiaries. PAPP DEV is not affiliated with or endorsed by Garmin.

[How we use data]

  • So that the coach can plan workouts, follow completed activities, compare planned and executed training, and adjust the athlete's plan.
  • To create and protect your account, and to send login emails and coaching team invitations.
  • To provide and maintain the service, provide support and fix errors.

Data is not sold, not used for advertising or profiling, and not sent to artificial intelligence or machine learning tools or platforms.

[Sharing]

  • Your coach: the athlete's training data and activities are visible to the coach of the coaching team the athlete belongs to, which is the purpose of the service. When an athlete leaves a coaching team, the coach no longer has access to new data.
  • Hosting: data is stored on a server contracted by PAPP DEV and located in Brazil, under PAPP DEV's control.
  • Garmin: only if you connect your Garmin account, we send Garmin the workouts scheduled for you, as described in "Garmin integration".
  • Google: used only to send transactional login and invitation emails, which contain your name and email. Google acts on our behalf and may process this data outside Brazil, as described in the general policy.
  • We may also share data when required by law or court order.

[Security]

  • All communication with the app and the web version uses HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as Argon2 hashes.
  • You can see your active sessions and end them at any time.
  • Access to servers and the database is restricted.

[Retention and deletion]

We keep data while your account is active. When you request account deletion, or ask us to delete your data, we delete it within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep. Withdrawing your consent stops the collection of new activities but does not delete what was already recorded; you can request that deletion at any time. Data received from Garmin is deleted within 30 days after you disconnect your Garmin account, as described in "Garmin integration". Database backups are kept for up to 14 days and then deleted automatically, so deleted data also stops existing in backups within that period.

[Your rights]

You can request confirmation of processing, access, correction, export (portability) or deletion of your account and data, information about sharing, and withdraw your consent at any time. To do so, email our data protection officer at the address below, from the email registered to your account.
