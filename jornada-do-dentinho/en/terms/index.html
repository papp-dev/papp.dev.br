What the app is

Little Tooth Journey helps parents and other caregivers follow a child's oral health, from pregnancy to the arrival of the permanent teeth: developmental milestones, brushing, appointments and educational content.

The content is educational

The texts, illustrations, videos and frequently asked questions are for educational purposes and were prepared based on scientific references. They do not replace a visit to a pediatric dentist, pediatrician or other health professional, and are not meant for diagnosis or treatment.

Every child develops at their own pace: the ages and time windows shown in the app are approximate. If the child has pain, fever, a dental injury or you have any doubt about their health, see a professional. In an emergency, seek care immediately.
