Frequently asked questions

I got a new iPhone. How do I get Premium back?

Open Settings → Premium → Your plan and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Settings → Premium → Your plan, tap Manage subscription. You can also do it in the iPhone Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and nothing you recorded is deleted.

How do I redeem an offer code?

In Settings → Premium → Your plan, tap Redeem code and enter the code.

How do I move my data to another device?

Turn on Settings → Sync → iCloud on both devices, using the same iCloud account. Your data goes to your iCloud account, privately, and shows up on the other device.

How do I export my data?

In Settings → Data → Export, the app creates a file with everything you recorded, so you can keep it or send it.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Settings → Data → Delete everything. If sync is on, you can also delete the copy stored in iCloud. Deleting can't be undone.

Does the app replace a dental visit?

No. Little Tooth Journey offers general guidance. For any question about your child's health, see a pediatric dentist.
