Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Little Tooth Journey is an app for parents and other caregivers to follow a child's oral health, from pregnancy to the arrival of the permanent teeth. This policy explains what data the app keeps, where it is stored and how you control it.

In short

  • Your data stays on your device. The app has no servers of its own and no user accounts.
  • If you turn on iCloud, the data is stored privately in your own iCloud account so it appears on your other devices.
  • The app does not collect usage data, does not show ads, does not track you and does not sell data. No one but you sees the data.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time, right in the app.

What data the app keeps

Only what you record yourself:

  • Children: name, date of birth or due date, gestational weeks at birth, gender and photo (optional).
  • Brushing: morning and evening records.
  • Appointments: date, type, clinic and professional name, and notes (optional).
  • Milestones: dates, notes, and the photos and videos you attach.
  • Preferences: reminder times, language and other app settings.

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

Where the data is stored

Everything is kept on your device, protected by iOS security features. We recommend using a passcode or Face ID on your device.

We, the developers, do not receive or have access to this data.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn it on in Settings → Sync → iCloud, the app keeps a copy of your data in the private database of your iCloud account (Apple CloudKit), so it stays the same on all your devices. Only your account can access it; we cannot. This storage is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To remove the iCloud copy, use Delete iCloud-synced data on the same screen. The data on your device stays there.

Premium subscription

The subscription is purchased through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and only tells the app whether the subscription is active on the device; we don't receive your card or Apple Account details.

Device permissions

The app only asks for a permission when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Notifications: for brushing, appointment and milestone reminders. Reminders are created on the device itself.
  • Camera: to take a photo of the child or of a milestone on the spot.
  • Photos: you choose through the iOS picker; the app only receives the photos and videos you pick.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Links to other websites

Scientific references and some content open third-party websites (for example, articles and dental associations). Those websites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Children's data

The app is meant to be used by the caregiver, not by the child. Whoever adds the child decides what to record, and that data stays under that person's control, on their own device. We handle this in the child's best interest, as required by data protection laws such as Brazil's LGPD and the European GDPR.

How you control your data

  • View and correct: everything can be edited in the app.
  • Export: in Settings → Data → Export, you can create a file with all your data.
  • Delete a child: the child stays in Recently deleted for 30 days, in case of a mistake, and is then permanently deleted.
  • Delete everything: in Settings → Data → Delete everything. This cannot be undone.
  • Delete from iCloud: see the iCloud section above.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the device (the iCloud copy, if any, stays in your account until you delete it).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with its update date. Important changes will be announced in the app.
